# 0.1.3 — annotated product-dimension fix

## Reproduction and root cause

Reproduced on the actual reported staging URL `/product/the-portable-folding-table/` with the 0.1.2 asset:

- Original: `Rectangle 30″ x 72″ (seats 6)`
- Bug: `Rectangle 76.2 cm x 72″ (seats 6)`

The quote-marker suffix guard accepted a dimension separator after the first inch symbol but rejected the opening parenthesis after the last symbol. This was a parser-boundary defect, not a page-builder node or stored-product-value problem.

## Fix

Permit parenthesis/bracket/brace annotations after explicit inch symbols only. Do not widen bare-English `in` handling. Keep quoted prose, feet compounds, and area/volume notation protected, including a parenthesized square-unit annotation. Seating counts are not interpreted as measurements.

Fixed output: `Rectangle 76.2 cm x 182.9 cm (seats 6)`.
